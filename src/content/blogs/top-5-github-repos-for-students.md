# How to Make Friends in College (Without It Feeling Awkward)

College is full of people — yet it’s easy to feel alone. Making friends doesn’t need to be intimidating. Here's how to make genuine connections.

## 1. Start With Shared Spaces

Common areas, clubs, dorm lounges, study groups — these are your social launchpads. Being present increases your chances of bumping into new people.

## 2. Ask Questions

People love talking about themselves. Try:
- "How did you like that lecture?"
- "Are you from around here?"
- "What clubs are you into?"

Simple conversations open doors.

## 3. Join a Club or Team

Whether it’s coding, debate, dance, or Dungeons & Dragons — join something that excites you. It gives you something to bond over.

## 4. Don’t Fear the First Move

Everyone’s just as nervous. Be the person who says “hi” first — it’s a small step that changes everything.

## 5. Consistency Builds Connection

Keep showing up. Trust builds over repeated interactions. It’s not about one deep talk — it’s about many small ones.

---

Friendship isn’t instant. But every great connection begins with a small step. Show up, reach out, and be yourself.
