# Mental Health Matters: Surviving the Emotional Rollercoaster of College

College is exciting, but it’s also overwhelming. Between deadlines, grades, relationships, and identity discovery, mental health often takes a back seat.

It’s time to change that.

## Recognizing Burnout

Symptoms include:
- Constant fatigue
- Lack of motivation
- Poor concentration
- Emotional numbness

It’s not laziness — it’s overload. Learn to spot the signs early.

## Building a Support System

Whether it's friends, mentors, or counseling services — don’t isolate. Talking helps, and you're not alone in your struggles.

## Daily Habits That Help

- **Sleep 7–8 hours**: All-nighters are not sustainable.
- **Eat real food**: Your brain needs fuel.
- **Exercise**: Even a 20-minute walk can elevate your mood.
- **Journaling**: A mental detox for your thoughts.

## Cut Down on Comparison

Social media can skew reality. You’re seeing curated highlights, not real-life lows.

## Learn When to Ask for Help

Counseling isn’t just for crisis. It’s like having a gym for your mind. Use your college’s mental health resources — they’re there for you.

---

Your mental health is not a distraction from success — it’s the foundation of it. Take care of yourself like you take care of your grades.
